# Code of conduct

Participation in this project must be respectful and professional. Discuss
technical decisions on their merits, explain disagreements, and accommodate
contributors with different backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation,
unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of another person's private
information are not acceptable. These expectations apply to issues, pull
requests, reviews and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation or remove
access when needed to protect participants. Responses should be proportionate
to the behavior and consider its impact, repetition and available evidence.
Maintainers are subject to the same expectations and should take no part in
decisions involving a personal conflict.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer contact listed in pyproject.toml:
[cevatbatuhan.tolon@gmail.com](mailto:cevatbatuhan.tolon@gmail.com). Include relevant links
and the context you are comfortable sharing. Reports are handled privately;
information is shared only as necessary to investigate and respond. This
project does not use Django's conduct team to administer its own reports.
